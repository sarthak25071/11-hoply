# 🎤 Hoply — 3 Minute Product Pitch

> **Tagline:** *Connecting travellers before connecting rides.*

---

# Speaker 1 (0:00 – 1:20)

Good afternoon everyone.

Let me ask you a simple question.

Imagine you've just landed at **Bengaluru Airport** after a long flight.

You're tired.

You collect your luggage and walk outside.

In front of you is a long queue of people waiting for taxis.

Some are travelling to Whitefield.

Some are going to Electronic City.

Some to Koramangala.

Now here's something interesting...

Many of those people are actually travelling to the exact same destination as you.

Yet...

every single one of them books a separate taxi.

The result?

- Higher fares
- Longer waiting times
- More traffic
- More pollution

Not because people don't want to share a ride...

but because they simply don't know **who else is travelling where they're going.**

Today's ride-sharing platforms like Uber and Rapido help you book a vehicle.

They don't help you discover a travel companion before the ride begins.

**And that's exactly the problem we wanted to solve.**

So, we built **Hoply**.

Hoply is a web application that connects nearby travellers heading toward similar destinations, allowing them to mutually decide whether they'd like to share a taxi.

Instead of matching people with cars...

**Hoply matches people with people.**

---

# Speaker 2 (1:20 – 2:20)

Using Hoply is simple and intuitive.

A traveller creates a travel request by entering:

- Their destination
- Preferred travel time
- Number of bags
- Luggage size

Once submitted, Hoply intelligently recommends nearby travellers who have similar destinations, overlapping travel times, and compatible luggage.

Each recommendation shows useful information like:

- Compatibility score
- Destination
- Estimated distance
- Luggage details
- Languages spoken
- Estimated fare savings

If both travellers are interested, they simply accept each other's request.

Once there's a mutual match, contact details are shared and they can book any taxi service they prefer and split the fare.

An important point is that **Hoply is not another taxi-booking application.**

We're not trying to replace Uber or Rapido.

Instead, we complement them.

Uber helps you find a cab.

**Hoply helps you find the right person to sit beside you in that cab.**

---

# Speaker 1 (2:20 – 3:00)

For our MVP, we intentionally focused on solving one problem really well.

Our application enables users to:

- Create a travel request
- Discover nearby compatible travellers
- View traveller profiles
- Send and receive match requests
- Confirm a shared ride after mutual acceptance

This demonstrates the complete journey from arriving at the airport to finding a travel companion.

In the future, Hoply can be expanded with features such as:

- Identity verification
- Real-time chat
- Women-only preferences
- Group travel
- AI-powered compatibility recommendations

But even in its simplest form, Hoply creates real value.

It saves money.

It reduces waiting time.

It lowers traffic congestion.

And it makes travel a little more social and a lot more sustainable.

Because at the end of the day...

**Hoply doesn't help you find a taxi.**

**It helps you find the person already travelling your way.**

Thank you.

# 🎯 Delivery Tips

## Speaker 1

- Start with a 2-second pause before speaking.
- Make eye contact with the audience.
- Tell the opening as a story rather than reading it.
- Pause after:
  - "Yet..."
  - "every single one of them books a separate taxi."

---

## Speaker 2

- Speak energetically while explaining the product flow.
- Use hand gestures when listing features.
- Emphasize:

> "Hoply is not another taxi-booking application."

Pause.

Then continue:

> "It helps people find people."

---

## Ending

Finish confidently with:

> **"Hoply doesn't help you find a taxi. It helps you find the person already travelling your way."**

Pause.

Smile.

Then say:

> **"Thank you."**
